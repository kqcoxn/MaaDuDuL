## **New Tasks**

When new tasks are added after an update, they will be appended to the end of the list.

#### **Usage**

1. **Move the task to the correct section**:
   - If it is a **routine task** and both the start and end screens are the main screen, move it into the "Routine Tasks" section.
   - If it is an **independent task** and its screens do not connect cleanly, move it into the "Independent Tasks" section.
2. **Configuration advice**:
   - After adding a new task, test it by itself first.
   - Once it is confirmed to work correctly, add it into your regular daily workflow.

#### **Notes**

- This separator is only meant as a temporary holding area. It is not recommended to keep tasks in this section long term.
