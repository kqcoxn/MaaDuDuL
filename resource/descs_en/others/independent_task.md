## **Independent Tasks**

Independent tasks are tasks that cannot be automatically chained together with other tasks. They usually have the following characteristics:

1. **Independent screens**: The start screen or end screen is not the main screen, so they cannot connect cleanly to routine tasks.
2. **Manual triggering is recommended**: They are usually better started manually and should generally not be inserted into an automated task chain.
3. **Special flow**: Their execution flow is more specialized and may require extra prerequisites or follow-up handling.

#### **Suggestion**

When configuring independent tasks, keep these points in mind:

- It is recommended to right click the task title and use the standalone run action.
- Do not place independent tasks inside a routine task chain.
